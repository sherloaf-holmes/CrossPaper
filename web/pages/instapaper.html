How do I get an API key?
  1. Sign in at instapaper.com/developers and create an application.
  2. Leave it in Owner Only mode. You don't need to submit it for review.
  3. Copy the consumer key and secret below. The secret is shown only once, when you create the application.

You need your own key because Instapaper revokes keys that are shared publicly, and only the key's owner can download article text without a paid plan.

Your password goes to Instapaper once, to get a sign-in token. It isn't stored on the device.
